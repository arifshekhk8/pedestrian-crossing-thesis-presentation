\opening{Dear Editor,}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled: \textbf{``Temporal Validity and Sampling Bias in Pedestrian Crossing Prediction: A Multi-Dataset Audit and Controlled Evaluation''} for consideration for publication in \textit{``Multimodal Technologies and Interaction''}.

This study examines the temporal validity of pedestrian crossing prediction using the PIE, JAAD, and IDD-PeD datasets. We conduct a frame-level audit to identify cases in which the crossing action is already present in the input observations, and we re-anchor the PIE samples to the observed crossing onset to remove this direct temporal overlap. We further examine how differences in temporal sampling affect model evaluation and compare BiLSTM, GRU, vanilla RNN, and Transformer models under the corrected protocols. Pedestrian bounding-box coordinates and ego-vehicle speed are considered the primary input modalities in the multimodal experiments. The results show that temporal sample construction can affect both model comparisons and modality ablation results.

We confirm that the manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not currently under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to \textit{``Multimodal Technologies and Interaction''}.


Thank you for considering our manuscript. We look forward to your response.
